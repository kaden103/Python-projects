## Game overview

Spider-Man takes on the Green Goblin across three rounds, with waves of the Goblin's gang in between:

1. **Round 1**: the Goblin patrols the sky and drops pumpkin bombs.
2. **Wave 1**: street thugs walk in to punch (or throw bottles if you hide on a ledge), plus a drone.
3. **Round 2**: the Goblin adds homing pumpkins and a glider dive. Watch for the red flash.
4. **Wave 2**: thugs and diving drones.
5. **Final round**: the Goblin is enraged, hunts you nonstop, and drops fire pumpkins that burn the ground.

Clearing a wave restores a heart. Beat all three rounds before losing your three hearts.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or arrow keys | arrows |
| Jump, climb up a wall | W or Up | JUMP or up arrow |
| Drop through a ledge, climb down | S or Down | down arrow |
| Web shot | Space: left/right shoots sideways, add W for diagonal, no direction aims at the nearest enemy (straight up at the Goblin) | WEB |
| Punch | F or J | PUNCH |
| Swing | E or Shift | SWING |
| Pause | P or Esc | tap to resume |
| Sound on / off | M | |

Jump into a wall to cling to it. Land on a thug or drone from above to stomp it.

## Features
- *Title, win and lose screens* with your score, time and best score (saved between games)
- *Score and combos*: chain hits within 3 seconds for up to x5, and hits made while airborne, swinging or on a wall count double
- *Aimable web shot* with light aim assist, and a punch for close fights
- *Ledges* you can land on, drop through, and hide under (pumpkins and bottles smash on them)
- *Game feel*: hit flashes, screen shake, hit-stop, particles and comic-book "WHAM!" words
- *Sound effects and chiptune music*
- *Phone and tablet support*: big on-screen buttons in a panel below the game (upright) or on both sides (sideways), and full screen on the first tap where the browser allows it

## Code layout (`SpiderMannn/`)
- `main.py`: entry point (pygbag runs this for the web build)
- `game.py`: scenes, rounds and waves, collisions, scoring, HUD
- `player.py`: Spider-Man movement, swinging, wall crawling, web shot
- `goblin.py`: the Green Goblin's phases, pumpkins and fire
- `enemies.py`: thugs, drones and bottles
- `effects.py`, `audio.py`, `controls.py`, `storage.py`, `settings.py`

The thug, drone and fire sprites and all sounds and music are generated by `tools/make_spiderman_assets.py`.

## Running
- Desktop: `pip install pygame`, then `python SpiderMannn/main.py`
- Web build: `pip install pygbag`, then `pygbag --build --template SpiderMannn/static/mobile.tmpl SpiderMannn`
